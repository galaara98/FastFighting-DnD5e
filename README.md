# FastFighting D&D 5e

> **AI use:** This module used AI in its creation.
> - **Programming:** I am a skilled programmer by profession, but AI was used heavily as a co-pilot.
> - **Strings:** ALL initial strings were proposed by a LLM to get the concepts out, ALL strings were  tracked to make sure they were re-written (for simple or ttrpg/game specific phrases, approved by) a native speaker.

Dungeons & Dragons Fifth Edition support for [FastFighting Core](https://github.com/galaara98/FastFighting), the Foundry VTT battle estimator, storyteller and resolver.

## What it does

FastFighting predicts how a fight will go, plays the creatures you don't want to run by hand, and writes every turn back onto the sheets. This module teaches it 5e: actions, bonus actions and reactions, advantage and disadvantage, conditions, grapples, spells, slots and concentration, Legendary Actions and Resistance, recharges, Extra Attack and Multiattack, death saves, Inspiration and summons.

It plays the 2014 or 2024 rules, as the world is set, and needs only the free SRD; other books' spells and features are read the same way.

## Why you'd want it

- **Before the fight:** see whether an encounter is trivial or deadly before your players find out.
- **During it:** let FastFighting run the monsters, or anyone whose player is away, rolled with dnd5e's own cards and damage application.
- **After it:** Rewind a turn or the whole encounter, sheets and chat included.

[How D&D 5e plays](docs/how-5e-plays.md) has every rule, setting and what isn't played.

## Requirements

- Foundry v14
- The `dnd5e` system, 4.0 or later
- FastFighting Core 0.14.0 or later
